Privacy Summary

A quick overview of how we handle your data

Privacy at Recalibrate

Your reflections are personal, and we take that seriously.

Journal entries

  • Your journal entries are private and only visible to you.
  • We do not use journal entries to train AI models.

Mood and body check-ins

  • Mood and body check-ins are used to generate personalized guided practices.
  • This data is also stored so you can reflect on patterns over time.

Guided practice feedback

  • Feedback you share on guided practices is linked to your account.
  • We use this feedback to improve future guided practices.
  • Journal content is never included in this process.

Your control

  • You may delete your account at any time.
  • Deleting your account removes your personal data from our systems.
