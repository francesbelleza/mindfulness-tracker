Privacy Policy

How we protect and use your information

Last updated: December 27, 2025

Recalibrate is a mindfulness web application designed to support reflection and self-awareness. We collect only the information necessary to provide this experience.

Information We Collect

Account Information

When you create an account, we collect basic information such as your email address to operate the app.

Journal Entries

  • Journal entries are private by default.
  • Journal content is visible only to you.
  • We do not use journal entries to train AI models.

Mood and Body Check-Ins

  • Mood and body check-ins are used to generate personalized guided practices.
  • This data is stored so you can reflect on patterns over time.
  • Mood and body check-in data may be processed by AI systems to create guided practices.

Guided Practice Feedback

  • Feedback you provide on guided mindfulness practices is linked to your user account.
  • We use this feedback to improve and refine future guided practices.
  • Practice feedback may be reviewed internally or used to improve AI-generated practices.
  • Journal entries are never included in this process.

How We Use Information

We use collected information to:

  • Provide and maintain the app
  • Display your personal reflections and history
  • Generate and improve guided practices
  • Ensure reliability and security

We do not sell your personal information.

Your Control Over Your Data

  • You may delete your account at any time.
  • Deleting your account removes your personal data, including journal entries, mood and body check-ins, and practice feedback, from our active systems.

Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your information, but no system can guarantee absolute security.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy as the app evolves. Updates will be reflected on this page.

Contact

If you have questions about this policy, please send us a message.
